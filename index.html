04 · House method

PROCESS, documented.

STEP 01

Excavate

Every brand is buried under bad defaults. Week one we dig: the audience, the rivals, and the story nobody bothered to tell.

→
STEP 02

Distill

One idea, boiled until it's dangerous. If a stranger can't repeat it back in five words, it doesn't ship.

→
STEP 03

Craft

Kerning, paper stock, pixels, pauses, all in equal measure. Obsession is the job description, not a bonus.

→
STEP 04

Hand over

Archived, labelled, documented. Files so tidy your next hire will assume the last designer cared. It was me.
